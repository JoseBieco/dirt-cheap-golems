Dirt Cheap Golems 2.0.0
=======================
 - New golems:
     End Stone Golem: flies, collects dropped items in its home radius and teleports them into a linked container (4 items per integrity)
     Gravel Golem: artillery that throws flint shards (16 blocks) and leaps to bury hostile mobs under falling gravel
     Basalt / Deepslate Golem: owner-bound shield that follows its owner, intercepts ranged threats and absorbs hostile projectiles
 - Golem Core command mode (sneak):
     Sneak + click an inventory block links it to the core; sneak + click the air clears the link
     Sneak + click a golem gives it an order (Basalt Golem: FOLLOW / GUARD, owner only)
 - BREAKING CHANGE: sneak-clicking a pillar with a Golem Core no longer creates a golem; use a normal click
 - Spawn eggs now use a single tinted model, one color pair per golem
 - New block tags: materials/end_stone, materials/gravel, materials/basalt
 - New config sections: end_stone, gravel, basalt
 - The Golem Core recipe now shows in the recipe book
 - Version bumped to 2.0.0
